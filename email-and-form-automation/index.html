How it works

Rosters, reminders and follow-ups, none sent by hand.

01 · Written once

Write each email once.

Booking confirmation, shoot-day reminder, images ready: write them once, and each one goes out on a date set from the photoshoot, with the client's name and date filled in and any flyer attached.

02 · Chased

One link for the roster, and it chases itself.

The roster request is a form with one link and one deadline. It reminds every 3 days, up to 5 times, until it's submitted. The client opens it with a one-time code: no login, and only their contacts get in.

03 · Seen

Know what's sent, delivered and back.

Every email shows sent and delivered, every form shows how many have submitted, and the person chasing it gets an email the moment one comes back.

Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center.

Who gets each email?

Everyone at the client who holds the role it's aimed at, like Yearbook. Add a contact to a photoshoot and that photoshoot's emails go to them instead.

Can I check an email before it goes?

Yes. Preview it with the client's name and dates filled in, or send yourself a test.

Can I send one early or again?

Yes. Send any email or form by hand from the photoshoot, to any contact at that client, when they ask for it again.

We send these from Gmail now. What moves over?

On your setup call we build your emails and forms into your photoshoot templates with you, so every photoshoot you book from then on sends them.
