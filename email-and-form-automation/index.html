Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center (opens in a new tab).

Who gets each email?

Everyone at the client who holds the role it's aimed at, like Yearbook. Add a contact to a job and that job's emails go to them instead.

Can I check an email before it goes?

Yes. Preview it with the client's name and dates filled in, or send yourself a test.

Can I send one early or again?

Yes. Send any email or form by hand from the job, to any contact at that client, when they ask for it again.

Do clients need a login to fill in a form?

No. They open the link and enter a one-time code sent to their email, so only their contacts get in.

We send these from Gmail now. What moves over?

On your setup call we build your emails and forms into your job templates with you, so every job you book from then on sends them.
