# SectionHeader

The eyebrow, heading and intro paragraph that opens every section of a page. Using it consistently is most of what makes a long marketing page feel designed rather than assembled.

Guidelines:

- Eyebrow in `eyebrow` style, `ink-muted`, preceded by a 6px `ink` dot at `radius-pill`.
- Heading in `display-l` for a page section, `display-xl` in the hero only. Keep it to one line on desktop where the copy allows.
- Intro paragraph in `body-l`, `ink-secondary`, around 68 characters per line, at most two sentences. If it needs three, the copy is doing the feature list's job.
- `space-3` between eyebrow and heading, `space-4` between heading and paragraph, `space-7` before the section's content.
- Left-aligned by default. Centre it only in the closing CTA band.
