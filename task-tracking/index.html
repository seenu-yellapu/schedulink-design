Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center (opens in a new tab).

Can a task wait for another one to finish?

Yes. Set it to start when another task on the same job is done. Its due date still counts from the job, so finishing early never moves the deadline.

Does every step have to be ticked?

No. Steps guide the work, they don't block it. Mark a task done with a step left over when it didn't apply, or finish every step and the task closes itself.

What about work that isn't in the template?

Add a task to any job at any time, even one already under way. Work that isn't tied to a job, like a quote, can sit on the client instead.

We track tasks somewhere else today. What moves over?

On your setup call we build your tasks into your job templates with you, so every job you book from then on comes with them.
