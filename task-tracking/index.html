How it works

Before and after every photoshoot, nothing slips.

01 · Created

Every photoshoot brings its own tasks.

Book a photoshoot and its pre- and post-production tasks are created with it, already assigned, each with a start and due date counted from the shoot. The person assigned is told when the task is ready to do.

02 · Done

A checklist inside every task.

Steps break each task down: cull, export, print, pack. Each step records who started it, who finished it, and when, so the history writes itself.

03 · Tracked

All the studio's work in one list.

Filter every task by due date, person or status. Overdue and due-today work is called out, and each person sees their own on their dashboard.

Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center.

Can a task wait for another one to finish?

Yes. Set it to start when another task on the same photoshoot is done. Its due date still counts from the shoot, so finishing early never moves the deadline.

Does every step have to be ticked?

No. Steps guide the work, they don't block it. Mark a task done with a step left over when it didn't apply, or finish every step and the task closes itself.

What about work that isn't in the template?

Add a task to any photoshoot at any time, even one already under way. Work that isn't tied to a shoot, like a quote, can sit on the client instead.

We track tasks in a spreadsheet. What moves over?

On your setup call we build your tasks into your photoshoot templates with you, so every photoshoot you book from then on comes with them.
