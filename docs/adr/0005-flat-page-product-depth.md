# 0005. The page is flat; the product has depth

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20.

## Context

- Shadows on every card make a page look assembled and heavy.

## Decision

1. No shadows on sections, cards, buttons or panels. Structure comes from line hairlines.
2. Product images, and only product images, get shadow-product; the front-most layer of a stacked image gets shadow-product-lift.

## Consequences

- The product stands out from the page without any colour.
- Open question: the ScreenshotFrame guidelines say fragments get no shadow (13 Roadmap, question 4).

## Alternatives

- Soft shadows on cards and panels.

## Revisit when

- The product imagery changes kind.
