# 0005. The page is flat; the product has depth

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20. Only the decision is carried over. Point 2 is narrowed by [0011](0011-fragments-without-shadow.md): screenshot fragments have no shadow.

## Decision

1. No shadows on sections, cards, buttons or panels. Structure comes from line hairlines.
2. Product images, and only product images, get shadow-product; the front-most layer of a stacked image gets shadow-product-lift.
