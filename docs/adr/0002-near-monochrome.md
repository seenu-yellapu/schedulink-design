# 0002. Near-monochrome, with one brand blue

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20. Only the decision is carried over.

## Decision

1. A grey page, white cards, greys, one house dark (#2F2F2F) and the brand blue. No second accent colour.
2. Blue only on the logo, buttons, links, the selected tab, section tags, the "New" label, product-card accents and the badge on a benefit symbol.
3. Attention is shown by weight, position, words and a 3px ink rule, not by hue.
