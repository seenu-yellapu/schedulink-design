# 0002. Near-monochrome, with one brand blue

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20.

## Context

- Studio owners compare several platforms; a calm page reads as a calm product.

## Decision

1. A grey page, white cards, greys, one house dark (#2F2F2F) and the brand blue. No second accent colour.
2. Blue only on the logo, buttons, links, the selected tab, section tags, the "New" label, product-card accents and the badge on a benefit symbol.
3. Attention is shown by weight, position, words and a 3px ink rule, not by hue.

## Consequences

- Hierarchy comes from type size and weight, so the full scale is used.
- Product screenshots are the only place with more colour.

## Alternatives

- A second accent for highlights.

## Revisit when

- A section needs a colour to be understood and words cannot carry it.
