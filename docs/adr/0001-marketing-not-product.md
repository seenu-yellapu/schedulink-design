# 0001. The marketing site has its own design system

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20.

## Context

- The app is dense because studio staff live in it all day.
- The site has about eight seconds to make a studio owner feel that ScheduLink is calm, modern and built by people who understand picture day.

## Decision

1. schedulink.io follows this system; the app and the mobile app follow the ScheduLink Product design system.
2. The two share the brand blue, the house dark and the three faces; nothing else is assumed to match.

## Consequences

- A change to the product system does not change the site, and the reverse.
- Where the site shows the product (tab pictures, fragments), it shows the product as it is, never restyled to the site.

## Alternatives

- One system for both: the site would inherit the app's density.

## Revisit when

- The product system and this one drift apart in a way visitors notice when they sign up.
