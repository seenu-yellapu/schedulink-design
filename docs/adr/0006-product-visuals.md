# 0006. Product visuals: illustrations and fragments, fictional data

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20.

## Context

- A full screenshot of a dense app at 40 percent scale proves nothing and reads as clutter.
- Real client or student data can never appear on a public site.

## Decision

1. Two kinds of product visual: the hero illustration, and tab pictures that are simplified but true views of real screens. Screenshots appear as cropped fragments, never full windows.
2. People are illustrated faces. All data is fictional, with one demo account and one fake season across every capture. Never blurred real data.
3. No stock photos, no illustrated blobs.

## Consequences

- Every claim on a picture has to exist in the app.
- Illustrations must be updated when the screens they show change.

## Alternatives

- Full app screenshots in a browser frame.

## Revisit when

- The app changes faster than the illustrations can follow.
