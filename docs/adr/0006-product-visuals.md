# 0006. Product visuals: illustrations and fragments, fictional data

## Status

Accepted (carried over). Decided in the ScheduLink Marketing design system before this repo existed; moved here unchanged on 2026-10-20. Only the decision is carried over.

## Decision

1. Two kinds of product visual: the hero illustration, and tab pictures that are simplified but true views of real screens. Screenshots appear as cropped fragments, never full windows.
2. People are illustrated faces. All data is fictional, with one demo account and one fake season across every capture. Never blurred real data.
3. No stock photos, no illustrated blobs.
