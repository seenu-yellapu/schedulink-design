Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center (opens in a new tab).

Can seasonal contractors use it?

Yes. Give a peak-season contractor a date range and leave out the days of the week they don't work. They get their own login once they accept your invite.

What does each photographer see?

Each photographer sees the photoshoots they are on, on their own dashboard, and can accept or decline from there.

Does it work with Google Calendar or Outlook?

Yes. Scheduled photoshoots go onto your connected calendar, so your team sees the week where they already look.

We use a spreadsheet and a group text. How do we move over?

Send us your staff list and we bring it in. Each photographer gets an invite and sets their own login, and once they have, the next week you publish reaches them by email and text.
