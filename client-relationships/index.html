Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center.

Can one person look after two clients?

Yes. Add them as a contact under each client, so each client's emails reach them.

What's the difference between a contact's title and role?

Title is their job, like Principal. Role is what they do for you, like Yearbook, and it decides which emails they get.

Can we send a client's details to our lab?

Yes. Download any client as a PDF with its contacts, their roles and your own fields.

What happens when a client stops booking?

Archive them. Their history stays, and it's all there when they come back.

We keep clients in a spreadsheet. What moves over?

Fill in two spreadsheet templates, one for clients and one for the people at them, and we import them before your setup call.
