Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center.

Does it stop a photographer being booked twice?

It flags it instead. Assign someone who is already on another photoshoot at that time, or off that day, and a warning appears next to their name with a link to the clash. The call stays yours.

Can one photoshoot run over several days?

Yes. It keeps one set of details, with its own photographers on each day, and its emails and forms go out once.

When do client emails start, and when does my team hear?

Nothing sends while a photoshoot is in Planning. Set it to Scheduled and its emails start and it goes onto Google Calendar or Outlook, if you've connected one. Publish it and every assigned photographer gets one email and one text.

What moves over from what we use now?

Your clients, the people at them and your staff, from simple spreadsheets. We import them before your setup call, then build your photoshoot templates with you on the call.
