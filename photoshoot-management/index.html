Questions

Asked at the booth.

More answers in the help center.

Does it stop a photographer being booked twice?

It flags it instead. Assign someone who is already on another photoshoot at that time, or off that day, and a warning appears next to their name with a link to the clash. The call stays yours.

If I change a template mid-season, what happens to photoshoots already booked?

The change reaches every photoshoot built from that template that is not complete yet. Finished tasks and emails already delivered stay as they were.

When do client emails start, and when does my team hear?

Nothing sends while a photoshoot is in Planning. Set it to Scheduled and its emails start and it goes onto Google Calendar or Outlook. Publish it and every assigned photographer gets one email and one text.

What moves over from what we use now?

Your clients and the people at them, from two spreadsheet templates. We import them before your setup call, then build your photoshoot templates with you on the call.
