Questions

Switching questions.

More answers in the help center (opens in a new tab).

What do we need to send you?

Your clients, the people at them and your staff, in our spreadsheets. Most studios take a few days.

Do we have to move everything at once?

No. Start with the part you need most and add the rest when you're ready.

Will my team need training?

Very little. Each person gets only the parts they need, so everyone sees a short, simple app, on their phone too.

Do we keep our calendar and gallery platform?

Yes. ScheduLink connects to Google Calendar, Outlook and Netlife, and you keep the gallery and ordering platform your clients already know.

Is setup extra?

No. Your import and setup call come with ScheduLink.

What if we want to leave?

There's no contract. ScheduLink is month to month, and if you leave you can export all of your data.
