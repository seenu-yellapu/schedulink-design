Do we have to move everything at once?

No. Start with the part you need most and add the rest when you're ready.

What does switching cost?

Nothing. The import and the setup call are always free.

Is there a contract?

No. ScheduLink is month to month and you can cancel any time. If you ever leave, you can export all of your data.

Yes. Start a 30-day free trial, no credit card, and invite your team to use it with your own clients.
